RNLOGO Regular

Install RNLOGO.ttf and type Ravi’s Notebook (or Ravi's Notebook), with standard ligatures enabled, to reproduce the full original wordmark.

For applications that do not support the brand ligature, type @ to insert the exact logo as one glyph. The logo is also at Unicode U+E000. Color is controlled by the application.

The original logo artwork is traced into vector outlines. Additional Latin support characters derive from condensed Bodoni Moda under the SIL Open Font License; see BodoniModa-OFL.txt.
Build source: tools/build_logo_font.py.

Version 2.0: complete engraved Latin uppercase and lowercase alphabet. Original logo glyph and ligatures are preserved. Rebuild with tools/complete_logo_alphabet.py; source at tools/font-sources/RNLOGO-v1.ttf.
