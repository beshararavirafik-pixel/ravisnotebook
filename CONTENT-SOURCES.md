Ravi confirmed permission to reproduce the source hymn texts, translations, word studies, and books on October 5, 2026. Available hymn languages and analysis are imported into local JSON files; permitted PDFs and EPUBs are stored locally. Original source URLs are retained as provenance metadata rather than visitor links. Credit to the source church is preserved in the readers.

No source recordings are imported or streamed. Ravi will provide recordings separately. The featured doxology uses the source English translation with the other imported languages; the earlier independent English study rendering is no longer used by its reader.

Ravi’s existing notes, feast URLs, and browser-local editing tools are preserved. Saved items are stored only in the visitor’s browser. No sign-in or server is required.

## Book reader dependency

The built-in PDF reader vendors Mozilla PDF.js (pdfjs-dist 6.4.299) under the Apache 2.0 license, included at `assets/vendor/pdfjs/LICENSE`. Fonts and other supporting assets retain their included licenses.

Only Ravi’s original lessons are included in Notes. The supplied external notes site was a reference for future functionality; its catalogue entries and links have been removed.
