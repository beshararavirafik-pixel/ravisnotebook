About this site

Freely shared,

This site collects hymn recordings, teaching notes from Sunday school lessons, liturgical texts, and resources gathered over years of serving the Church. Everything here is freely shared. Nothing is sold. If something helps you pray better or understand your faith more deeply, that is enough.
