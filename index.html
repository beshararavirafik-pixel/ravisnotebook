About this site

"A personal effort to make Coptic Orthodox learning more accessible — for youth, seekers, and anyone who wants to grow deeper in the faith of our fathers."

This site collects hymn recordings, teaching notes from Sunday school lessons, liturgical PDFs, and resources gathered over years of serving the Church. Everything here is freely shared. Nothing is sold. If something helps you pray better or understand your faith more deeply, that's enough.
